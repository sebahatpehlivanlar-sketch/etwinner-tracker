eTwinner Tracker

One helpful forum reply earns one red apple.

How it works
  1. In the monthly TwinSpace forum, answer a partner group’s question or suggest one clear improvement. Start with a group from another country that’s waiting for feedback.
  2. Each helpful reply earns one apple for the school that wrote it. The pupil editor selects their school and group, then adds the reply link. Each link counts once.
  3. Each tree holds 24 apples. Open a school’s extra trees to see apples earned after 24. Country totals add up the apples from all schools in that country. Ties are shown.
  4. Records and school settings are saved in this browser. Export a copy and import it into the browser your team uses to merge records. The tracker does not sync automatically.
